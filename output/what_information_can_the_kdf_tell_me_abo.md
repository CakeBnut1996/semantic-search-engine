# Search Result: What information can the KDF tell me about the state of bioenergy in 2026?

## 🧠 Answer Summary

The KDF provides a variety of data sets and reports that together describe the current and projected state of bioenergy in the United States, including forest‑based feedstock, marine and algae fuels, and the environmental impacts of expanding biofuel production. The primary‑forest‑resources dataset (BT2) contains yearly raw outputs for 2012‑2030, which can be used to estimate forest biomass availability in 2026. Other collections such as the Billion‑Ton Study key‑topic overview, the macro‑algae data set, and the 2016 Billion‑Ton Report volume 2 give context on marine biofuels, emerging marine feedstocks, and environmental effects of biomass expansion. No single document explicitly states the bioenergy situation in 2026, but the combination of these resources allows users to infer the 2026 outlook.
